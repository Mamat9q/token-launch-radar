How the radar works

The value model — radar-2

implied = TVL × 0.25 × (1 + clamp(7d, −60, +120) ÷ 100 × 0.5) × (0.6 + min(age, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4) × (1 + 0.02 × min(chains, 5))

Four figures, all from the snapshot. A quarter of TVL is the base: that is the size of the protocol that would have to be rewarded. Growth adds up to +60% or takes away up to 30%. Age adds up to +40% for a protocol that has survived three years. Each extra chain adds 2%, capped at +10%. No protocol name is ever special-cased — the same function scores every row, and the detail view shows each factor for the protocol you are looking at.

Who counts as pre-token

A protocol is on this board only when its record carries no token at all — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol — and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. Excluded: Bridge, Canonical Bridge and Cross Chain Bridge products, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking and Restaked BTC, exchanges, names ending in a version marker, and names carrying the whole word wrapped, staked or staking. That is why WBTC, Binance staked ETH and Coinbase Bridge are not here: they are Bitcoin and ETH in another form, not protocols waiting on a launch of their own.

What “records no token” means

The snapshot records what DefiLlama lists. A row on this board records no token in that snapshot — that is not proof the protocol has none, and it is not a promise that it will ever launch one. Implied value is a model, not a price target and not financial advice. Farm plans are a checklist, not a guarantee of an airdrop.

What is set aside

The Live token tab lists the protocols that already trade, by market cap. They are shown with the market's own values and never mixed into the ranking: a token that already trades has no launch left to farm.
